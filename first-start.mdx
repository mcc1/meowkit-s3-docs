# Powering on

press and hold the power button for about 1–2 seconds. Once powered on, the screen and system interface will appear automatically.

![Power On](/images/power-on.png "Power On")

<Warning>
  Notes

  - If the device does not turn on, try charging it for a few minutes first
  - Avoid repeatedly pressing the power button during startup To power off the device, press and hold the power button for about 3–4 seconds until the shutdown screen appears.
</Warning>

# Rebooting

Briefly short the `RESET` and `GND` pins on the GPIO header with a jumper wire or Dupont pin to reboot the device automatically.

![Rebooting](/images/rebooting.jpg "Rebooting")

# **SD Card Insertion**

Make sure a microSD card is inserted into your MeowKit before using storage-related features or updating firmware. The microSD card is used to store apps, assets, configuration files, media, logs, and other user data.

MeowKit supports FAT16 and FAT32 file systems and is compatible with most microSD / microSDHC cards. For the best compatibility and performance, we recommend using a standard 8–32 GB FAT32-formatted microSD card.

Higher-capacity cards may require longer initialization times and can vary depending on card quality and formatting. If the card is not recognized properly, please reformat it to FAT32 before use.

![SD Card Insertion](/images/SD-Card-Insertion.png "SD Card Insertion")

<Warning>
  **Notes**

  You need a microSD card to use the device

  MeowKit doesn't come with a microSD card, so you'll need topurchase one separately.
</Warning>

# **Copying the bundled SD files**

Several apps read their data from the card. The firmware repository ships that
data in the `sd files/` folder; copy its **contents** to the root of the card
before the first use:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Format the card as FAT32 (8–32 GB recommended).">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy everything inside `sd files/` from the firmware repository to the root of the card.">
    After copying, the card root must contain `assets/`, `badusb/`, `infrared/`, `music/` and `vu_meter/`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the infrared library.">
    `/infrared/universal/` must contain the `.ir` files (`tv.ir`, `ac.ir`, `audio.ir`, …). Without them the
    Infrared app's **Universal (all brands)** menu shows "Universal library not on SD card". Remotes you learn
    yourself are stored next to that folder as `/infrared/<Device>.ir`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

# **Update Firmware**

Keep your MeowKit up to date with the latest official firmware. The update runs directly from your browser.

Before you begin, make sure MeowKit is **powered off** and the USB cable is disconnected.

<Steps>
  <Step title="**Enter Download Mode.**">
    Insert a Dupont jumper wire through the hole on the back of MeowKit until it presses the **BOOT** button. Keep holding BOOT, then press and hold the **Power** button for **1–2 seconds** and release it. Keep BOOT pressed throughout, then remove the jumper wire. The screen will remain off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Connect MeowKit.**">
    Use a **USB Type-C cable** to connect Meowkit to your computer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Open the download page.**">
    Go to [https://meowkit.cc/pages/download](https://meowkit.cc/pages/download) and click "**Connect and install**".
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Select the serial port.**">
    Choose **USB JTAG/serial debug unit (COMxx)**, such as COM16, then click "**Connect**".
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Select the firmware.**">
    Click "**Install Meowkit S3**".
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Confirm the installation.**">
    Check "**Erase device**", then click "**Next**".
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Start the update.**">
    Confirm the installation and click "**Install**" to begin the firmware update.
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Wait for completion.**">
    Wait while the installation progress reaches 100%. When "Installation complete" appears,

    your Meowkit is ready to use.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Keep MeowKit connected throughout the installation. **Erase device** will remove stored settings and data.
</Note>
